FedEx and UPS size rules

Large or heavy boxes pay extra fees. Some fees also set a minimum billable weight.

Source: FedEx Service Guide and UPS Rate and Service Guide, 2026 editions. From December 21, 2026, UPS raises its Additional Handling minimum from 40 lb to 50 lb.

Checked against each service's maximum weight, longest side, and length + girth.

How it is measured. Length is the longest side. Girth is 2 × (width + height), using the two shorter sides. DIM weight is length × width × height ÷ divisor, rounded up to the next pound. You are billed for the greater of DIM weight and actual weight, rounded up to the next pound.

Carrier rules. From the FedEx Service Guide 2026, the UPS Rate and Service Guide 2026, the USPS Domestic Mail Manual (November 2026 issue) and DHL eCommerce's 2026 product terms. Checked November 18, 2026. Carriers change these rules, and your agreement can set a different divisor or minimum. Use this page as a guide, not a quote.
